Privacy Policy

This policy describes the HuntAlong website and early-access interest list. The HuntAlong beta app uses location and routing services. The app and website have different data practices, described below. HuntAlong is operated by an individual developer in California.

Early-access form

When you join the interest list, we collect the email address you enter, an optional first name, and your consent choice. Our form provider, Formspree, processes and stores form submissions on our behalf. We use this information to manage interest in HuntAlong and send occasional beta and launch updates. A beta invitation is not guaranteed.

Contact messages

If you contact HuntAlong by email, we use the information you provide to respond to your message and handle support requests.

Beta app

The separate HuntAlong beta app can use entered locations, routes, and device location (when permission is granted) to provide route and nearby discovery. In the reviewed v0.9.45 app source, favorites, visit history, visit counters, and active-hunt information are saved in local device storage using AsyncStorage. This information is not sent to a HuntAlong-operated account server by those storage functions. Device backups and third-party platform services may handle device data separately. The app makes requests to third-party mapping and place-search services to provide discovery and routing. Please contact support@huntalong.app for questions about the beta app.

Google Maps

HuntAlong uses Google Maps Platform for place and routing information. Google may receive data needed to process requests, including search or location information. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Maps Additional Terms.

Website technical information and service providers

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser requests to deliver the site. We use Google Analytics to understand website usage, including page views and interactions. Google Analytics may collect browser, device, approximate location, and interaction information and use cookies or similar technologies. The early-access form is processed by Formspree as described above.

Do Not Track

The website does not implement a separate response to legacy browser Do Not Track signals. Google Analytics may use cookies and related browser identifiers; you can use browser privacy controls to limit tracking. Other parties providing website hosting or form processing may receive technical information in connection with your visit or submission. This does not override legally required privacy preference signals.

Retention and removal requests

Interest-list submissions are retained in the Formspree account while needed to administer early-access communications, subject to deletion requests and applicable legal requirements. No fixed retention period has been established. To request access, correction, or removal of information you submitted, email support@huntalong.app. We will review requests and respond as required by applicable law.

Children

The early-access interest list is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, contact us to request removal.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or practices change. Questions can be sent to support@huntalong.app.
