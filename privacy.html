Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current HuntAlong beta. It will be reviewed and updated before public release as the app's services and features are finalized.

Information used by HuntAlong

HuntAlong uses location and route information when you ask it to find stores near you or along a route. This can include a location you enter, a destination you enter, or your device location when you choose to use the current-location feature.

The app may also store app preferences and activity needed for features such as favorites, trip planning and visit history on your device.

Third-party mapping and place services

HuntAlong uses Google Maps Platform services to provide functions such as place search, place information and routing. Information needed to perform those requests may be transmitted to Google and is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Contact and support

If you email HuntAlong for support or feedback, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue.

Children

HuntAlong is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a HuntAlong account system. The current beta does not provide a HuntAlong user-account registration feature.

Changes

This policy may change as HuntAlong develops. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
