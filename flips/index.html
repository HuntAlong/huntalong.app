FLIP #1

The green-trim Wii

Found at Goodwill

Tagged$14.99
Sold$500

Most Wiis are all white. This one had green trim, so we flipped it over and checked the label.

It said Development Tool (RVT-002): a Nintendo developer unit. It sold in a 7-day auction for $500.

FLIP TIP
Odd colors or trim on a console? Always flip it over and check the label.
