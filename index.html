Your next treasure hunt is already on your route.

Already heading somewhere? HuntAlong helps you discover thrift stores and other treasure-hunting stops along your route—not just near you. Compare extra driving time, choose your stops, and make more of the journey.

Simple by design

Plan. Discover. Hunt.

Whether you're heading across town or taking a road trip, HuntAlong is designed to help you find worthwhile stops without losing sight of your route.

1

Choose your route

Enter where you're starting and where you're going, then choose how much of a detour you're willing to make.

2

Find treasure stops

Find thrift stores and other treasure-hunting destinations along the drive, with estimated detour time for each candidate.

3

Build your hunt

Add stops to your trip, keep track of favorites and visits, and hand off to navigation when you're ready to go.

See a real example

One drive. Two treasure stops.

Here's a real trip planned in HuntAlong: Napa to Stanford with a 15-minute max detour. Every stop shows its extra driving, and your shopping time is counted separately, so you know exactly when you'll arrive.
